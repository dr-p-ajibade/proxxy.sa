Where things go wrong

  • Records spread across inboxes and shared drives, with no plan behind them.
  • Funding and tender deadlines met with rushed, last minute proposals.
  • Staff who were never shown how to file, keep or share information.

Each of these costs money in failed audits, missed bids and hours lost searching. We fix them one at a time, starting with whichever hurts most.

Four services that work on their own or together.

Proposals and tenders

We write and edit proposals, tender responses and grant applications for government agencies, NGOs and firms bidding for public work.

Staff training

Online or on site, with materials staff keep using after the session.

Records management consulting

File plans, retention schedules, records audits and a register of what you hold and where it sits.

Questions clients ask first

Do you work outside South Africa?

Yes. Proxxy Media started in Budapest and works with clients in Africa, Europe and elsewhere. Consulting and training run online, and on site visits can be arranged.

Can you help with a tender or grant that closes soon?

Put the closing date in the form. We will tell you honestly whether there is enough time to do it well before we take it on.

Where does our data go?

Automations run on infrastructure you can control, and we write down exactly what data moves where. Records work follows POPIA, GDPR or your local law, and we are happy to sign a confidentiality agreement before we see anything.

What does it cost?

The consultation is free. Every project is quoted as a fixed price once the scope is agreed, and ongoing support is a fixed monthly fee. You see the numbers before you commit to anything.
