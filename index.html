Why it matters

Good information governance is no longer optional

Records, data protection and new technology now depend on each other. Get the first two right and the third becomes safe to adopt.

AI is only as safe as your filing

Two thirds of organisations have delayed or cancelled Microsoft Copilot over concerns that it could surface confidential files. Clean records and permissions come first.

Source: CoreView, State of Microsoft 365 Security and Governance 2026

Regulators are checking

In South Africa, only about half of public bodies filed their annual PAIA report this year, and the Information Regulator is now issuing formal enforcement notices.

Source: Information Regulator figures, reported by Polity, September 2026

Some filings now come every year

In Nigeria, organisations that process the personal data of more than 1,000 people in six months must file a data protection compliance audit return every year.

Source: Templars, Data Protection Compliance in Nigeria, January 2026
