Questions firms ask first

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with the email, accounting and document tools you already use, and only suggest something new when it saves more than it costs.

Where does our client data go?

Automations run on infrastructure you can control, and we write down exactly what data moves where. Records work is designed with POPIA in mind, and we are happy to sign a confidentiality agreement before the audit.

What does it cost?

The audit is free. Builds are quoted as a fixed price once the scope is agreed, and the retainer is a fixed monthly fee. You see the numbers before you commit to anything.

Do you only work with South African firms?

Most of our work is with South African firms, and we also work with firms in Nigeria. Everything is done online.
