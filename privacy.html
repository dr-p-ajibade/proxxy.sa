Privacy notice

Last updated 22 October 2026

This notice explains how Proxxy Media handles the personal information you send us through this website, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, for clients in Europe, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect

When you use the consultation form we collect what you need help with, your name, organisation, email address, type of organisation, country, and anything you choose to add: your phone number, a note about your work, and whether you want RegisterIQ early access.

Why we collect it

We use it only to reply to you, arrange your consultation and, if you ask, tell you about RegisterIQ. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Where it is kept

Your request is sent to our business mailbox and a copy is stored on our web hosting account, both with Hostinger. Only Proxxy Media can see them.

How long we keep it

If you do not become a client, we delete your details within 12 months of your last contact with us. If you do, they become part of your client file and follow the retention period agreed with you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing info@proxxymedia.com. If you are not happy with how we handle it, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa or, in the EU, to your national data protection authority.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
