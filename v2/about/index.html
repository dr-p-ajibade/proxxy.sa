About

Expert led, from scope to delivery.

Proxxy Media was founded in Budapest and now works from South Africa with clients across Africa, Europe and beyond.

Who leads the work.

Dr Patrick Ajibade is a library and information science scholar, researcher and consultant. His research covers how organisations adopt technology, how they manage records, and how AI changes information governance. His most cited paper, on the technology acceptance model, has 632 citations.

The person who scopes your project is the person who delivers it. Larger projects bring in trusted specialists, always named in your agreement.
