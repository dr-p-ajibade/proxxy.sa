Why it matters now

Good governance is no longer optional.

AI adoption

AI is only as safe as your filing

Two thirds of organisations have delayed or cancelled Microsoft Copilot over concerns that it could surface confidential files.

Source: CoreView 2026, via Infosecurity Magazine

Regulation

Regulators are now checking

Only about half of South Africa's public bodies filed their annual PAIA report this year, and the Information Regulator is issuing formal enforcement notices.

Source: Information Regulator figures, via Polity, September 2026

Research

Publicly funded research must now be open

South Africa approved a National Open Science Policy in December 2025, and a data management plan is how researchers meet it.

Source: Stellenbosch University Library, May 2026
