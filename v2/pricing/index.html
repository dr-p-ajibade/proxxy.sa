Billing

Simple, in writing.

Book a free consultation
How does billing work?
After the free consultation you get a written agreement with your monthly fee. We invoice monthly in advance, and you can pay by EFT or debit order.
Can I change plans?
Yes. Move up or down a plan, or add and remove add ons, from the next billing month.
Why are prices shown as 'from'?
The price depends on your size, how many systems you use and how much is already in place. You see the final price in writing before you commit.
Do prices include VAT?
No. Prices exclude VAT where it applies. Dollar prices are a guide for clients outside South Africa and are invoiced in US dollars.
Is this legal advice?
No. We give compliance and information management support. Where you need a legal opinion, we work alongside your attorney.
